  across the zone row in the render — the card is ~207cm wide and 4.8cm tall.

  Fix I'd apply

  Take the card mount out of the flex layout: keep a zero-content FlexItem in
  the column purely to reserve stackHeightCm() of vertical space, and parent a
  separate StackMount SceneObject directly under this.sceneObject (outside
  content), positioned at the reserved band. getStackRoot() returns that
  instead. Cards then inherit only the zone's uniform 1.09 scale, and
  stackCardScale behaves as documented.
